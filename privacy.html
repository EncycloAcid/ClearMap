Clearway privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Clearway is an airport map app. It is built so that nothing about you leaves your phone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

What we collect

Nothing. Clearway has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send any information anywhere.

Location

If you tap the location button, Clearway asks for permission to use your location, so it can show where you are on the terminal map and give directions from there. Your location is used only on your phone, only while the app is open, and is never stored or shared. You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Notifications

If you ask for a reminder to head to your gate, Clearway asks for permission to show a notification and schedules it on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

What is saved on your phone

To pick up where you left off, the app keeps a few settings on your device: the airport you chose, your gate and boarding time, recent searches, and display choices. They stay on your phone and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Links

The map credit links to OpenStreetMap's website, which opens in your browser and is covered by that site's own policy.

Children

Clearway is suitable for everyone and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
