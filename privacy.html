Clearway privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · updated for live flight status

Clearway is an airport map app. It is built so that as little as possible leaves your phone, and nothing that identifies you. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

What we collect

Clearway has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The map, directions and search work on your phone without the internet.

Live flight status and security waits

If you track a flight, the app sends the flight number, today's date and the airport's code to the Clearway Live server, which looks the flight up with our flight-data provider (AeroDataBox) and sends back its status, gates and times. To show security wait times, the app sends only the airport's code, and the server looks it up with our wait-time provider (TSAWaitTimes.com).

These requests contain no name, account, location or device identifier. The server keeps answers in a short-lived cache (a few minutes) so repeated lookups are faster, does not log flight numbers, and does not store anything about you. Like any website, the providers and our hosting provider (Cloudflare) see the server's requests and may keep standard technical logs under their own privacy policies. All of it travels encrypted (HTTPS). The flight you're tracking is saved only on your phone, and you can stop tracking at any time.

Location

If you tap the location button, Clearway asks for permission to use your location, so it can show where you are on the terminal map and give directions from there. Your location is used only on your phone, only while the app is open, and is never stored or shared. You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Boarding pass scanning

If you tap the scan button, the Android app opens Google's code scanner (part of Google Play services) to read the barcode on your boarding pass; Clearway itself does not get access to your camera. In the web version, you choose a photo and your browser reads the barcode. The barcode is read on your phone. Clearway keeps only the flight number, date, seat, boarding group and whether the pass shows TSA PreCheck, saved on your phone with the flight you're tracking; your name and booking reference are ignored and never saved or sent. As when you type it, only the flight number and date are sent to look up the flight.

Notifications

If you ask for a reminder to head to your gate, Clearway asks for permission to show a notification and schedules it on your phone. If you turn on the gate countdown, a notification with your gate, boarding time and when to leave stays on your lock screen until boarding; it's made on your phone from the trip you're tracking. Nothing is sent to a server.

What is saved on your phone

To pick up where you left off, the app keeps a few settings on your device: the airport you chose, your gate and boarding time, recent searches, and display choices. They stay on your phone and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Links

The map credit links to OpenStreetMap's website, which opens in your browser and is covered by that site's own policy.

Children

Clearway is suitable for everyone and does not collect information from anyone, including children.
