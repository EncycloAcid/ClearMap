Clearway is a calm, clear map of the inside of the airport: where your gate is, how long it takes to walk there, and what's on the way.

AIRPORTS
• Cleveland Hopkins International (CLE)
• Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta International (ATL), including the Plane Train
More airports are on the way.

WALKING DIRECTIONS
• Turn-by-turn steps from security to your gate, with walk times and distances
• Go mode: one step at a time, with a turn arrow and the time left
• Upper and lower levels, with escalators, elevators and the Plane Train marked
• Step-free routes that use elevators instead of escalators

JUST LANDED?
• Arrivals mode takes you from your gate to baggage claim, then to the train, rideshare, taxis, rental cars or the pickup curb

PASS THE TIME
• See how much time you have before boarding, and which restaurants, coffee shops, bars and shops you can visit and still make it back
• Opening hours, so you don't walk to somewhere that's closed (Cleveland)
• A reminder when it's time to head to your gate

FIND ANYTHING
• Search gates, airlines and places, even with everyday words like "pizza" or "bathroom"
• Nearest restroom, coffee or food in one tap
• Every airline and which concourse it uses

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline: the app doesn't use the internet at all
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your location, if you turn it on, stays on your phone

Light and dark themes. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Gates, hours and airline concourses change. Always check your boarding pass and the airport's screens.
